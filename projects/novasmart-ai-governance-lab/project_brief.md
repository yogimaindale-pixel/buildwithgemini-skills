# Project Brief: NovaSmart AI Governance Lab

## Overview
NovaSmart AI Governance Lab is a security steering framework designed for the **Head of AI Platform & Security**. It provides visibility, access control right-sizing, content safety screening, and compliance audit log verification across NovaSmart's enterprise agent fleet.

## Core Missions & Design
- **M0: See Everything**: Shadow agent discovery across Cloud Run, GKE, and Agent Engine.
- **M1: Take Action**: Right-sizing identity privileges, fixing shared service accounts, and enforcing least privilege.
- **M2: Guardrails & Screening**: Content safety screening and PII protection.
- **M3-M5**: Audit logging, governance scorecard generation, and policy enforcement.
